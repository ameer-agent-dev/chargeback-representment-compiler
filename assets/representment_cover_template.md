## 6. Formal Written Cardholder Withdrawal Letter Template

Use this template whenever a customer verbally agrees by phone to withdraw a dispute. Card networks reject verbal phone promises and require a signed written statement from the cardholder.

```text
Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>

To: Card Issuer Dispute Resolution Department
Re: Written Withdrawal of Chargeback Dispute

I, <Cardholder Full Name>, confirm that I authorized the transaction of $<amount_usd>
with <Merchant Name> on <transaction_date> (Order #<order_id>) on my card ending in <last4>.

The matter has been resolved directly with the merchant, and I hereby formally withdraw
my chargeback dispute for this transaction. Please cancel the dispute and release funds
back to the merchant.

Cardholder Signature: ___________________________
Printed Name: <Cardholder Full Name>
Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
```
